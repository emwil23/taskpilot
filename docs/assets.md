# TaskPilot assets

All art was generated on 19 October 2026 in the owner's Google Flow project **"Revamp"**: stills with Nano Banana Pro, clips with Omni 1.1 Flash. The owner's master character appears **only** on the Meet the developer page and the landing page's developer card; everything else is product art with no people. The logo, favicon, PWA icons and UI icons are SVG drawn in code (`src/components/Logo.tsx`, `public/favicon.svg`, Lucide).

Shared style for every prompt: _premium 3D render, deep night-blue background (#0D1017), signal-orange (#FF6B3D) and sky-blue (#7CC4FF) rim light, glossy rounded glass task cards, calm, Apple-keynote style, no text, no logos._

## How they were optimised

- Stills: `sharp`, WebP quality 78, resized to the sizes above. `og.jpg`: mozjpeg quality 84, with the logo, name and tagline composited as SVG.
- Clips: `ffmpeg`, H.264 CRF 27 (`+faststart`) and VP9 CRF 38, 30 fps, no audio.
- Clips load with `preload="none"` and play only while on screen (`src/components/LoopVideo.tsx`), and never with reduced motion. The service worker doesn't precache them.
